3. Aim 1 — can 16S predict function? (superseded)

Aim 1 and its PICRUSt2 comparison were redone as a benchmark on PICRUSt2's own reference (DESIGN §B, reports/bench/). The pilot versions are in git history (commit 57d6ab1). What the pilot showed: accuracy rises with 16S identity to the nearest reference genome; learned models on allele features did not beat 5-NN; amplicons lose 9–35 % of the gain over the null; the head-to-head with PICRUSt2 was a tie overall, on unequal references.

4. Aim 2 — 16S alleles associated with function beyond phylogeny

1,795 bacteria, 3,252 alleles (3,106 unique patterns), 4,616 phenotypes (4,233 KOs, 383 modules), pyseer LMM with GTDB-tree kinship.

  • Calibration is good. Median genomic inflation λ is 0.94 (KOs) and 0.99 (modules), with 10–90 % range 0.6–1.2.
  • The positive control works. Reverse gyrase (K03170, hyperthermophily) has 187 Bonferroni hits, including the top 12 overall. Its positively associated alleles are 74 % G/C, against 47 % background: the known GC–thermostability relation of rRNA stems, recovered without being told.
  • 11,591 Bonferroni hits (2,518 phenotypes, 2,272 alleles); 20,801 at BH-FDR < 0.05. 10.7 % of tests were flagged by pyseer and excluded.
  • The homoplasy filter does not discriminate. All 2,272 hit alleles change more than once on the tree (Fitch). At 1,795 genomes almost every allele is homoplasic, so "> 1 change" separates nothing. Many hits are likely still clade-tagging that the kinship term absorbs only partly. A stricter criterion is needed before reading individual hits as biology (§7, question 3).

Calibration of the association test. One λ per phenotype; λ = 1 means the phylogenetic correction removes the inflation caused by shared ancestry, above 1 means leftover structure (false positives), below 1 means conservative.

Takeaway: both distributions centre on 1 (65 % of KOs between 0.8 and 1.2), so the test is not inflated by ancestry. The KO tail below 0.5 means a conservative test for those KOs: less power, not false positives.

Where the significant alleles sit along the 16S (E. coli numbering; grey bands = variable regions V1-V9).

Takeaway: hits spread along the whole gene and are not enriched in variable regions (43 % of hits, against 48 % of tested alleles). The highest peaks sit near position 125 (conserved stretch between V1 and V2), at the end of V2 (~235) and in V5 (~830-860). Positions with very many hits mark clades rather than single functions, which is why the homoplasy criterion (§7, question 3) matters.

6. What must change before full scale

  1. Aim 2 cannot run at 52k bacteria as built. Each phenotype runs pyseer separately. Each run re-reads an n × n kinship (≈ 30 GB as text at 52k) and re-decomposes it (O(n³)). Even with pyseer's --save-lmm / --load-lmm (decompose once), each phenotype costs O(n² × variants): about 1 CPU-h per phenotype at 52k, or ~4,600 CPU-h in total. Options: run Aim 2 on a stratified subsample (~15k bacteria: ~5 min per phenotype, ~12 h on 32 cores), plus --save-lmm.
  2. PICRUSt2 used 61.5 GB for 2k queries. Resolved in the benchmark design: PICRUSt2 runs per fold (DESIGN §B).

None of these touches the per-genome steps, which take most of the time.

7. Open decisions for the full run

  1. Scale. All 54,963 filtered genomes: ~7 days of eggNOG, ~260 GB of work disk. Or a stratified 20k subset: ~2.5 days. The case for 55k is that accuracy depends on reference density. Deferred until the benchmark (B5).
  2. Aim 2 sample (§6.1): stratified subsample of about 15k bacteria, or another size.
  3. Aim 2 homoplasy criterion (§4). Options: require ≥ 5 independent origins of the allele, or require the association to replicate within ≥ 2 independent orders (test within clades).
  4. PICRUSt2 with our reference. Decided 2026-11-06: done the other way round, both methods on PICRUSt2's own reference (DESIGN §B).
  5. Still open from the design: D048 (fixed hyperparameters, no inner CV), D060 (GC-enrichment control instead of a stem map), and D019 (Aim 2 bacteria only; the pilot has 204 archaea).
